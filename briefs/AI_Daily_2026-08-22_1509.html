Two threads dominate today: harness beats model, and money keeps moving toward whoever sits in the middle. Nvidia's own benchmark work and Latent Space's harness retrospective make the same point from different angles — scaffolding, memory management, and skill retrieval now explain more of agent performance than the underlying weights do. Worth internalizing before the next model-upgrade budget request.

Anthropic and OpenAI are each staking policy and security ground too — Claude Mythos 5 moving into cyber defense, OpenAI's new "AI Futures" blog on power and governance.

One caution for any AI-fluency rollout: the China homework-vs-exam study is a clean example of assistance improving the metric you measure while quietly eroding the skill you actually wanted.
