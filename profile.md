# Reader Profile

Senior pharmaceutical IT executive (VP/CTO-level) leading the AI strategic pillar at a major pharma company based in Prague. Responsible for AI strategy, digital transformation, and enterprise technology decisions.

## Role & Context
- Leading AI transformation in a regulated healthcare/pharma environment
- Managing teams delivering AI implementations across drug discovery, clinical operations, and enterprise automation
- Accountable to C-suite on AI ROI, risk governance, and regulatory compliance
- Operating across European and global pharma footprint from Prague HQ

## Reading Interests (Priority Order)
1. **AI-for-science / pharma**: Drug discovery AI, virtual cells, foundation models for biology, AlphaFold-like releases, clinical trial AI, regulatory AI submissions
2. **Enterprise AI deployment**: Concrete deployments at named organizations, adoption metrics, ROI numbers, production lessons
3. **Frontier model developments**: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Mistral, Meta — especially capability jumps relevant to enterprise/science
4. **AI capital flows**: Funding rounds, hyperscaler deals, compute capex, restructured contracts — signals of where the industry is heading
5. **Agentic systems**: Multi-agent frameworks, AI coding assistants at scale, autonomous workflows, enterprise orchestration
6. **Governance & workforce**: EU AI Act, FDA AI guidance, workforce displacement/upskilling, executive decision-making on AI
7. **Builder methodology**: Engineering write-ups with numbers, benchmark results, architecture decisions, cost/latency data
8. **Executive/management thinking**: How AI leaders restructure organizations, manage AI risk, make build-vs-buy decisions
