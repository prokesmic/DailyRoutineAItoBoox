# Reader Profile

The reader is a **senior pharma IT executive** currently leading the **AI strategic pillar** at a major pharmaceutical company based in **Prague**.

## Professional Context
- Leads AI strategy and implementation across pharma IT
- Accountable for enterprise-scale AI deployments
- Operates at the intersection of IT governance, vendor relationships, and scientific computing
- Manages budget for AI tooling and infrastructure

## Listening Habits
The reader regularly follows:
- **Latent Space** — technical depth, practitioners, research distilled for builders
- **The AI Daily Brief** (Nathaniel Whittemore) — market context, funding, lab moves
- **Everyday AI** (Jordan Wilson) — applied/enterprise AI, accessibility

## What He Values
- Strategic framing over feature announcements
- Enterprise deployment reality vs. vendor hype
- Capital and compute economics (who's spending, who's cutting)
- AI-for-science and pharma-adjacent developments (highest priority)
- Governance and regulatory signals that affect enterprise risk
- Builder write-ups with real numbers (cost, latency, adoption)
- Frontier lab moves that shift competitive landscape

## What He Skips
- Consumer AI product reviews
- Beginner explainers and listicles
- Crypto/blockchain crossover
