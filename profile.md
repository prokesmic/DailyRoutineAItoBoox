# Reader Profile

Senior pharma IT executive based in Prague, Czech Republic, leading the AI strategic pillar for a global pharmaceutical organization. Focused on translating frontier AI capabilities into measurable enterprise outcomes: reducing clinical trial timelines, accelerating drug discovery, improving manufacturing yield, and modernizing HCP engagement.

## Interests and Reading Habits

**Podcasts:** Latent Space, The AI Daily Brief, Everyday AI  
**Blogs/Newsletters:** Ethan Mollick (One Useful Thing), Andrej Karpathy, Zvi Mowshowitz, Pragmatic Engineer, Stratechery

## What Matters to This Reader

1. **Pharma/life-sciences AI** — virtual cells, protein/molecule foundation models, AlphaFold-class releases, clinical-trial AI, regulatory (FDA/EMA) AI guidance. High priority.
2. **Enterprise AI deployment** — named organizations, concrete scope, adoption metrics, what it cost and what it saved.
3. **Frontier model developments** — capability jumps, architectural shifts, benchmark results, from primary sources (OpenAI/Anthropic/DeepMind/Meta/Mistral/NVIDIA).
4. **Capital and infrastructure** — hyperscaler deals, data-center capex, GPU/TPU supply, funding rounds with strategic significance.
5. **Agentic systems** — multi-agent architectures, tool use, autonomous workflows in enterprise contexts.
6. **Governance, regulation, workforce** — EU AI Act implementation, US executive actions, layoffs/re-orgs with AI causation, CHRO/CPO responses.
7. **Builder methodology** — token economics, latency/cost tradeoffs, eval frameworks, internal rollout learnings.
8. **Executive strategy** — named CEOs/CTOs on AI operating models, board-level AI decisions, org design.

## What to Skip

- Consumer app reviews and app-store roundups
- Vendor press releases with no new technical or business claim
- Generic "AI is transforming X" explainers without concrete evidence
- Crypto/web3 crossover
- Beginner tutorials and prompt-engineering tips
