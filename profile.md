# Reader Profile

Senior pharma IT executive leading the AI strategic pillar at a major pharmaceutical company, based in Prague.

## Professional Context
- Accountable for AI strategy across the enterprise pharma IT stack
- Investment decision-maker for AI platforms, infrastructure, and vendors
- Bridge between frontier AI capability and regulated pharma operations
- Operates in EU regulatory environment (EMA, GDPR, IVDR, clinical data governance)

## Podcast Diet
- **Latent Space** – deep technical dives, AI research, infrastructure, builders
- **The AI Daily Brief** (Nathaniel Whittemore) – market moves, funding, enterprise trends
- **Everyday AI** (Jordan Wilson) – practical AI deployment, enterprise workflows

## What Moves the Needle
1. Concrete enterprise deployments with adoption metrics — not announcements
2. AI-for-science breakthroughs with pharma/bio angle (protein models, virtual cells, clinical AI, materials discovery)
3. Capital flows and compute economics — who is spending what and why
4. Regulatory and governance developments affecting enterprise AI in the EU
5. Agentic systems reaching production readiness
6. Frontier lab strategic moves with downstream enterprise implications
7. Builder write-ups with real numbers (cost, latency, adoption %)

## What to Skip
- Consumer-focused product reviews
- Vendor PR with no new technical claim
- Generic "AI is transforming X" think-pieces without evidence
- Beginner explainers
- Crypto crossover
- Incremental benchmark improvements without real-world context
