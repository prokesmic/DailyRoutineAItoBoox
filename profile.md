# Reader Profile

Senior pharma IT executive, leading the AI strategic pillar at a large pharmaceutical company based in Prague.

## Role & Context
- Responsible for AI strategy across the organisation: tooling decisions, build-vs-buy, infrastructure investment, org-design for AI capability
- Sits at the intersection of IT leadership and business strategy; reports to C-suite, advises on AI investment sizing
- Europe-based: GDPR, EU AI Act, and local talent markets are live concerns

## Reading Diet
Regular sources: Latent Space, The AI Daily Brief (Everyday AI), Ethan Mollick (One Useful Thing), Andrej Karpathy, Zvi Mowshowitz, Pragmatic Engineer, Stratechery.

## What he cares about most
1. **AI for drug discovery / pharma science** — virtual cells, protein structure, foundation models for biology, AlphaFold-line releases, clinical-trial AI tools
2. **Enterprise deployment reality** — named companies, actual adoption numbers, real cost/latency data, not vendor press releases
3. **Capital flows & compute** — hyperscaler deals, capex commitments, GPU allocation, data-centre builds; tells him where the weight of the industry is going
4. **Frontier model moves** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, Mistral primary sources; not press rewrites
5. **Agentic systems reaching production** — what's working, what's failing, architecture decisions
6. **Governance & workforce** — EU regulation, named executive decisions, restructurings with headcount
7. **Builder methodology with numbers** — token economics, eval scores, latency vs cost trade-offs, internal adoption %
8. **Executive / management thinking** — CEO/CTO perspectives on operating model, org design, AI transformation

## Down-rank signals
- Consumer gadget reviews
- Vendor PR with no new factual claim
- Generic "AI is changing X" without evidence
- Beginner explainers
- Crypto / web3 crossover
